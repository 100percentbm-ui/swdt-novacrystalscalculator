How it works

Every super rebirth adds the same flat amount, so the total grows in a straight line. At rebirth 40 the in-game numbers fit this exactly: 5 → 18,084 · 10 → 35,604 · 25 → 88,164 · 50 → 175,764 · 100 → 350,964.

Calibrate for a different rebirth

Open the super rebirth screen at your rebirth level and enter any two amounts it shows. The page works out the rate and saves it in this browser.
